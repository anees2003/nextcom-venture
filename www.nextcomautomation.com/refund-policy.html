Nextcom LLC Refund Policy

We understand that unforeseen circumstances may arise, and we are committed to working with you to achieve a satisfactory outcome. This refund policy outlines the conditions under which a refund may or may not be granted.

Services Not Refundable:

  • Discounted Packages: Due to the reduced pricing of discounted packages, refunds are not available.
  • Approved Concepts & Revisions: Refunds will not be granted after a primary design concept has been approved or revisions have been requested.
  • Urgent Projects & Self-Inflicted Cancellations: Urgent design projects and cancellations due to reasons unrelated to Nextcom LLC's services are non-refundable.
  • Client Unresponsiveness: Inactivity exceeding two weeks during the project will void refund eligibility.
  • Policy Violations: Violations of Nextcom LLC's policies will result in denied refunds.
  • Duplicate Projects: Engaging another designer/company for the same project eliminates your refund rights with Nextcom LLC.
  • Incomplete Briefs & Major Changes: Refunds will not be granted for projects where the creative brief lacks crucial information or requires a complete design overhaul.
  • Late Refund Requests: Claims submitted beyond the designated "request for refund" timeframe will not be considered.
  • Business Changes: Refunds are not applicable in cases of business closure, name changes, or other internal client restructuring.
  • Non-Service Reasons: Reasons like "change of mind," disagreements with partners, or personal matters are not grounds for a refund.
  • Processing Fee: A 12% processing fee will be deducted from all client-initiated refund requests.
  • Final File Delivery: Refunds are not available after final files are delivered based on your request.
  • Free Items: Refunds and revisions are explicitly excluded for complimentary items.
  • Specific Services: Services like Social Media, SEO, Domain Registration, and Web Hosting are non-refundable.
  • Website Development: Website development refunds are denied once the design is approved and development begins.
  • Logo Finalization: Logo revisions with positive client feedback and finalized files eliminate refund eligibility.
  • Third-Party Work: Refunds will not be offered for work outsourced by Nextcom LLC.

Eligible Refunds:

  • Service Delivery Failure: You may request a refund if Nextcom LLC fails to deliver the agreed-upon services, provided you fulfilled all prerequisites beforehand.

Refund Processing:

  • Refund requests will be reviewed within 30 business days (if applicable).

We encourage open communication throughout the project. If you have any concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us. We will work our best to find a solution that meets your needs.
